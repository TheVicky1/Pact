# PACT design system

PACT uses dark graphite, warm ivory, restrained amber, and quiet cool atmospheric light. Glass signals hierarchy and focus, never a blanket treatment.

- Canvas `#090a0c`; text `#f5f1e8`; muted `#aaa69f`; accent `#d3a866`.
- Surfaces use translucent white at 2.5–9% with a 1px white border at 7–11%.
- Geist provides display, body, and numerical clarity.
- Use 16px navigation controls, 24px content cards, and rounded pills for focused actions.
- Motion is 150–220ms and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- All controls are semantic, keyboard reachable, visibly focused, and retain readable contrast.
